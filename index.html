Based in Stockholm, Bahram Bader is a student at Södertörns Högskola. He studies the program IT, Media and Design. He is studying to design interactive design on the web and also making traditional designs. The current project is a virtual sword fight that is played by two people. This project was coded by hand and by generative ai.
